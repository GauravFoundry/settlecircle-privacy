SettleCircle Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

SettleCircle is an Android app for tracking shared expenses, household bills and money you lend or borrow. This page explains what the app stores and how you can delete it.

Works without an account

You can use SettleCircle with no account. In that case everything you enter (circles, expenses, lending records, receipt photos, your display name) stays on your phone. We never receive it.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and only needed to share circles with other people. When you sign in we receive, through Google and Firebase Authentication, your name, email address and a Firebase user ID.

When you share a circle, the following is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so the other members can see it:

  • your name, email address and the display name you use in that circle
  • the circle’s expenses, amounts, splits, settlements and activity
  • your responses to expenses (for example “looks right” or “questioned”) and any messages you post
  • invitations you create

Receipt photos are not uploaded. They stay on your phone.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for advertising or analytics.

Who can see what

Members of a shared circle can see that circle’s data, including the names and email addresses of the other members. Nobody else can read it.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to photograph a receipt. It is optional.
  • Notifications: for payment and lending reminders you set.
  • Internet: only used when you sign in or share a circle.

Deleting your account and data

Open the app, go to About, then Account & sync, and tap Delete account and cloud data. You will confirm with Google once more, and then:

  • your sign-in is deleted
  • your invitations, your responses and your user record are deleted
  • shared circles where you are the only member are deleted completely
  • in circles with other people you are removed, and ownership passes to another member

Records stored only on your phone are not affected. Remove them by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

If you cannot open the app, email stha.gaurav51@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my SettleCircle account”, and we will delete it.

Data location and security

Cloud data is stored with Google Firebase and is protected by access rules that limit each circle to its members. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Children

SettleCircle is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
